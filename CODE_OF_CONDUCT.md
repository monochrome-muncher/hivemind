# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Hivemind is a small project, and we want it to stay a place where it's
easy to ask questions, point things out, and suggest changes. Be kind,
be direct, and assume good faith. That's most of it.

## How we treat each other

- Be respectful, even when you disagree. Critique the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most mistakes here are honest ones.
- Give feedback clearly, and take it well when it's aimed at you.
- Keep the discussion about the project. Politics and religion are
  welcome in your life; in our issues, keep them out.
- If you're new, ask anything. If you see someone else asking, answer
  the way you'd want to be answered.
- If a thread is getting heated, check your tone, and step back if you
  can.

## If it goes wrong

If something lands wrong, open an issue or send a DM to
`@monochrome-muncher`. Say what happened and where; a link is ideal.
We'll look into it, keep it between us if you want, and follow up with
you. Complaints get a fair hearing, and we take them seriously.

If you're on the receiving end of a complaint and want to talk it
through, say so in the issue or the DM.

## Where this applies

Anywhere the project meets people: issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any chat we set up.
